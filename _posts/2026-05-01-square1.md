In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

this is a completely self-made blog powered by jekyll, my linux machine, and the lovely github pages.
super minimal, super simplistic. and i think that's the way it should be from now onwards.

_can't function, i'm fed up with this nonsense_
_lotta shit, and it's weighin' on my conscience, oh_

that's an excerpt from conscience, a track from the rapper Gunna.
and that's exactly what made me make this website.

i was suspended again on the blog that i was writing, this time on wordpress.
before, it was substack. and now, it's this.

i've decided enough is enough, and i'm not letting another platform suspend me, even if temporarily.
it's ridiculous and each time, there hasn't been a good reason to deplatform me.

it isn't a big deal, but the website being down for an indefinite amount of time, while lacking the bare minimum respect of letting me know in advance, or even notifying me about the suspension, that isn't going to cut it for me.

jekyll is the second ruby related project that i have laid my hands on. maybe i might learn ruby someday. the first was homebrew, and i can't say how much i love homebrew. it's an amazing piece of software.

this is the message i want to leave the ones who are reading this:
your words and actions have more impact than you think they have.
everything you've ever wanted was on the other side of fear.
and of course, the fear of you moving in the unknown is almost always better than living in regret.

i've got a lot of exciting stuff coming up.
In Sha Allah, you will be the first to see all of them!
